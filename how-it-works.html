THE REVIEW METHOD

Show the factors. Keep the call yours.

Submit a valid 15-player squad and Sidekick assembles a transparent first-pass report. A human reviewer can then add their own feedback before preparing a reply.

The local prototype uses a saved FPL data snapshot. It is a decision aid—not a points guarantee, official FPL feature or substitute for checking news before the deadline.

THE CHECKS

What the first pass looks at

01 · VALIDATE

Confirm the squad rules

Checks for 15 unique players, 2 goalkeepers, 5 defenders, 5 midfielders, 3 forwards, the £100m budget and a maximum of three players from one club.

02 · PLAYER TRENDS

Read recent and season output

Shows form, next-gameweek expected points, season points, minutes, goals, assists, clean sheets and availability notes for every squad member.

03 · FIXTURE RUN

Compare the next five gameweeks

Displays each player’s upcoming home/away opponents and official FPL fixture difficulty. The starting XI’s average difficulty gives a quick outlook.

FROM SUBMISSION TO REPLY

A direct request, then a human review.

When the squad and email are submitted, the page calculates the report and sends the review request to the Formspree recipient configured for FPL Sidekick. The visitor stays on the page. The reviewer can add personal advice and then send a reply to the manager; that human reply is still prepared and sent manually. The demo Review Desk remains local to the current browser and does not sync across devices.

THE MANAGER

Build and submit

Pick the XI and bench, choose a formation, and provide an email address. The squad and first-pass review go to the configured inbox through Formspree.

THE REVIEWER

Add your own judgement

Review the email, add personal feedback, and manually send a thoughtful reply to the manager.

BEFORE GOING LIVE

Host and protect the workflow

The site still needs public hosting. The demo Review Desk has no login or shared queue; add a real privacy notice and private review backend before relying on it across devices.
